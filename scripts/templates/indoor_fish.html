Indoor Fish密室遊魚

Chrollo's Indoor Fish can only live in a sealed room. You are standing in one with them. It holds ten of Togashi's manuscripts, and the two fish eat each one as he draws it. A fully eaten room is a finished batch. When the batch begins in Weekly Shōnen Jump, the room is opened and the fish are gone.

Eaten = drawn, from Togashi's production posts on X.
