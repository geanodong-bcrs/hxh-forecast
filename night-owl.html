Posting time is not necessarily working time

We have 498 archived posts from his account, covering 2022-05-24 to 2026-10-15. Taken together, they do not look especially nocturnal. The average posting time is 17:27, 55% arrive between 09:00 and 18:00, and only 13% arrive between 22:00 and 06:00.

But the two large spikes are public routines. Togashi posted near noon during his 2022 page-log run, then near 17:00 during the 2024 run. Those timestamps tell us when he updated X, not when he drew.

His posting schedule keeps changing

Plotting every post over time makes the routines visible. The dots form a noon line in 2022 and a 17:00 line in 2024. Outside those periods—especially since 2025—posts are scattered from midnight through the evening.

The colours show production stages: lighter blue is earlier work, darker blue is closer to a finished manuscript, and orange is not a production-progress report. There is no stable relationship between a production stage and the hour it is reported. A manuscript-completion post may arrive in the afternoon, evening or after midnight.

So the timestamps are a poor guide to when Togashi works.
