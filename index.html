What the stages mean

StageJapaneseWhat happens
StoryboardネームRough layout: panel structure, rough figures, where the dialogue sits.
Inking人物ペン入れTogashi inks the characters by hand, in pen.
BG brief背景指定書Written instructions telling the assistants what backgrounds to draw.
